ABOUT TOOLFORGE

Useful tools, without unnecessary steps.

ToolForge is an independent web project focused on practical online utilities for everyday tasks.

What we build

ToolForge brings practical everyday utilities into one clean place: unit and currency conversion, word counting, password generation, image compression, and PDF tools.

Privacy by design

Where practical, file and text operations run directly in your browser. Live currency rates use an external service because current exchange-rate data must be fetched online.

Our goal

We want ToolForge to grow into a dependable library of genuinely useful free tools that work well on desktop and mobile without unnecessary sign-ups.

Transparent monetization

ToolForge may use policy-compliant advertising or other transparent monetization methods in the future. We do not want misleading buttons, fake downloads, or deceptive interactions.

ToolForge is an independent project and is not affiliated with Google, Microsoft, Adobe, or other third-party services mentioned on the site.
