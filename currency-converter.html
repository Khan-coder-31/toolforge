What is currency conversion?

Currency conversion is the process of changing an amount from one currency into another. For example, if you have money in US dollars and want to know its value in euros, you can convert USD to EUR using an exchange rate.

People use currency converters for travel, international shopping, online services, business transactions, budgeting, and many other everyday tasks. A currency converter makes the calculation easier because you only need to enter the amount and choose the two currencies.

How do exchange rates work?

An exchange rate tells you how much of one currency is equivalent to a unit of another currency. For example, a rate can be used to calculate how many euros correspond to a given amount of US dollars.

The important thing to remember is that exchange rates are not fixed forever. They can move over time as currency markets change. This is why a conversion result can be different when you check it later.

Tip: For planning or comparison, always check the rate and conversion result close to the time you need it. Banks, cards, and money transfer providers may also apply their own fees or exchange-rate margins.

Why do currency exchange rates change?

Currency values can change because of many economic and market factors. Interest rates, inflation, economic conditions, trade, investor demand, political events, and expectations about future economic conditions can all influence currency markets.

Because of these changes, a currency conversion result should be treated as a current estimate rather than a permanent value. If you are making an important payment or transfer, check the final rate and any applicable fees with the provider you are using.

Useful currency conversion tips

1. Check the currency codes

Make sure you selected the correct source and target currencies before converting. Similar-looking currency names can otherwise lead to mistakes.

2. Compare the rate with your provider

A currency converter can help you understand the exchange value, but a bank, card issuer, or money-transfer service may use a different rate and may charge additional fees.

3. Remember that rates change

If you are checking a conversion for a future trip or purchase, remember that today's result may not be the same later.

4. Use a calculator for quick comparisons

When comparing prices in different currencies, convert the same amount into one currency so that the prices are easier to compare.

Frequently asked questions

What is a currency converter?

A currency converter is an online tool that calculates the value of an amount in one currency using an exchange rate for another currency.

Can I convert USD to EUR online?

Yes. Select USD as the source currency, EUR as the target currency, enter the amount, and run the conversion.

Are currency exchange rates always the same?

No. Exchange rates can change over time, so a result can differ when you check the same currencies at another time.

Is an online currency converter the same as a bank's final rate?

Not necessarily. Banks, cards, and money-transfer services can use their own exchange rates and may add fees or margins.
