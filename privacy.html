Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

ToolForge ("we", "our", "the site") provides free browser-based utilities: a unit converter, currency converter, word counter, password generator, image compressor, and PDF merge/split tools. This page explains what happens to your data when you use them.

Files and text you enter

The image compressor, PDF merge, and PDF split tools process your files entirely inside your own browser using JavaScript. Your files are never uploaded to our servers or any third party — they never leave your device. Closing the tab clears everything.

Text you type into the word counter or password generator is likewise processed locally in your browser and is not sent anywhere.

Data sent to external services

Two tools call external, third-party APIs to function:

  • Currency Converter — sends the amount and the two currency codes you select to the Frankfurter exchange-rate API (frankfurter.dev) to fetch a live rate. No personal information is included in this request.
  • Fonts — this page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log standard request data (such as your IP address) as part of serving the font files.

Cookies and advertising

If ToolForge displays advertising (for example, through Google AdSense), Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data and manage your ad preferences at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand overall traffic (for example, how many people visit a page). This does not identify you personally.

Children's privacy

ToolForge is a general-audience utility site and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. Continued use of ToolForge after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact information provided on the ToolForge Contact page. During the project preparation stage, a dedicated support email will be added before public launch.
