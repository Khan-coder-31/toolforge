What is a word counter?

A word counter is a tool that calculates the number of words in a piece of text. Many word counters also show character counts, making them useful when a writing task has both word and character limits.

Students, writers, bloggers, editors, researchers, content creators, and social media users can use a word counter to check text length before submitting or publishing their work.

Why use an online word counter?

An online word counter makes it easy to check text without manually counting every word. You can paste or type your content into a browser-based tool and get a quick count.

  • Check an essay or assignment word limit.
  • Measure the length of an article or blog post.
  • Check characters for forms and social media limits.
  • Review transcripts or other large blocks of text.
  • Quickly compare the length of different drafts.
Tip: If a task specifies a maximum or minimum word count, check your text before submitting it so you can make any necessary edits.

Using a free word counter for writing

A free word counter can be useful whenever you need a quick text-length check without installing software or creating an account. For many everyday writing tasks, seeing the word and character totals is enough to keep a draft within the required limits.

Students and assignments

Essays, reports, applications, and assignments often have word limits. A free word counter can help students check their draft before submitting it.

Blog posts and articles

Writers can use word counts to compare drafts, plan article length, and make sure a piece of content is not significantly shorter or longer than intended.

Social media and short-form content

Character counts can be just as useful as word counts when a platform or form has a character limit.

What is a text word counter?

A text word counter is a word-counting tool designed to analyze a block of written text. Depending on the tool, it may provide word count, character count, and other basic text statistics.

This can be especially helpful for long text such as articles, transcripts, notes, essays, and drafts where estimating the length by eye is difficult.

Word counting tips

1. Check the requirement first

Different assignments and platforms may define their limits differently. Always follow the specific instructions for the task.

2. Check characters as well as words

If a form gives you a character limit rather than a word limit, use the character count to make sure your text fits.

3. Remove unnecessary text

If your draft is too long, remove repetition and unnecessary wording while keeping the important information and meaning.

4. Keep readability in mind

Reaching a particular word count should not be the only goal. Clear, useful, and readable writing is more important than adding words simply to increase a number.
